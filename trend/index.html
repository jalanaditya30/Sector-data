How to read this

Score = annualised log drift × consistency², where consistency is the efficiency ratio (net move ÷ total distance travelled, 0–1). A high score means the move was both meaningful and travelled in a straight line.

Last 10 sessions — one bar per trading day, oldest on the left. Bars rise above the centre line on up days and fall below it on down days; bar height is the size of that day's move (topping out around ±2.5%). The shaded block on the right is the most recent 5 sessions — the window that drives the turning call. Hover any bar for its date and exact move.
