How to read this

A 10–15% pop is already on Twitter by the time you could act on it. What gets missed is the stock adding 0.8% a day, most days, for six weeks. That is what this finds — by counting, not by fitting curves.

up days — sessions closing higher than the day before. 23/30 is a grind; 16/30 is a coin flip that happened to end higher. best run — longest unbroken streak. typical day — median daily move ignoring direction; under about 1% is the quiet kind. biggest day — largest single move; if it is a big share of the 30-day total it was an event, not a climb. last 5 — up days in the most recent week, to catch one rolling over.

last 30 sessions — one bar per trading day, oldest on the left, rising above the centre line on up days and falling below on down days. Bar height is the size of the move. The shaded block on the right is the most recent 5 sessions. Hover any bar for its date and exact move.
