Looking for stocks that rise a little, most days, for weeks — the ones no one posts about. Two numbers decide it: how many of the last 30 sessions closed up, and whether the gain came in small steps or one jump.
symbol up days ↓ best run 30d % typical day biggest day last 5 turnover cr verdict daily moves
up days — sessions closing higher than the previous close. 23/30 is a grind; 16/30 is a coin flip. best run — longest unbroken streak of up days. typical day — the median daily move, ignoring direction. Under about 1% is the quiet kind. biggest day — largest single move. If this is a big share of the 30-day total, it was an event, not a climb. last 5 — up days in the most recent week, to spot one that is rolling over. Bars run oldest to newest, up above the line, down below.
